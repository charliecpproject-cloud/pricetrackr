# PART 1 — Foundation

## What this is
Private, single-user price tracker for Japan (JPY), bilingual JA/EN product data, installed as a web app on Android. Manual entry + price lookup with history. No AI, no other users yet. Phase 1 is complete and live on Vercel.

## Built (Phase 1 and early extras)
- Next.js on Vercel + Supabase (Auth/DB), owner-only account (sign-ups disabled), RLS on every table.
- Tables: products, product_variations, product_packages, store_chains, shops, price_observations, profiles, app_settings, plus store_chain_usage, product_barcodes, search_aliases, brand_aliases, counter_units, favorite_packages, search_suggestions and the archive table deleted_records.
- Pages: home, price entry (with inline new product/package/branch), price check (latest price per chain), product detail (latest price, history, unit price), stores (chains, branches, coupons, membership and points settings), products (add/edit products, variations, packages, memo), delete own price entries, a barcode scan page (/scan), settings (/settings) and admin tools (/admin).
- Also built: chain coupons, membership price and bonus points, product variations, product memo, tax basis, unit price (on the detail page and on the price check cards), bulk discount tiers, ウェル活, barcode scanning, demo mode and roles, themes, search with aliases, counter-word units, toast notifications, admin tools, app icon and web app manifest.

## Not built yet (OUT)
AI/photo extraction, freshness/trust scoring, translation-approval workflow, internet auto-research, multi-user, shopping list, alerts, gamification, community/regional features. See PART2/PART3 when relevant — don't build ahead of scope.

## Schema principles (don't flatten these)
- Product ≠ package: `products(id, name_ja, name_en, brand, category, memo (optional free text, max 1000 chars))` vs `product_packages(id, product_id, variation_id (optional), package_size, package_unit)`. A multi-pack is entered as one total size (6 × 350 ml = 2100 ml).
- Variations: `product_variations(id, product_id, attributes jsonb [{name, value}], attributes_key)`, user-defined and optional. `attributes_key` is canonical (NFKC, trimmed, lower-case, order-independent) and unique per product. A package may belong to a variation of its own product (composite FK `(variation_id, product_id)`). Prices always reference the exact package, so each package has its own history. To make different sizes share one history, use one package and put the detail in the price note. Helpers live in `lib/products/variation.ts`. Units: ml, L, g, kg, count (個), sheet (枚), bag (袋). Nested selects must follow real foreign keys: `price_observations` to `store_chains` (direct) and `shops`; `product_packages` to `products` and `product_variations`; `products` to `product_variations` and `product_packages`.
- Store chain ≠ shop: `store_chains(id, name, coupon_percents int[] default '{}', allowed values 5/10/15, member_discount_percent smallint null = no membership price, has_bonus_points bool default false, point_use_enabled bool default false, point_use_multiplier numeric(4,2) default 1.5, bulk_enabled bool default false)` vs `shops(id, store_chain_id, name, address, region)`. Chains also have `category` (a key from `lib/stores/category.ts`: supermarket, drugstore, convenience, discount, hundred_yen, apparel, other; null = 未分類). `store_chain_usage(store_chain_id, entries_last_month, updated_at)` is a lookup table of price entries per chain over the last 30 days (by observed_at), recounted by `refresh_store_chain_usage()` when the price entry page finds it empty or older than 7 days; it drives the price entry chain dropdown (grouped by category, most used first, top 10 unless すべて is ticked). Chains and branches with price entries cannot be deleted.
- Price = observation, not attribute: `price_observations(id, product_package_id, store_chain_id (required), shop_id (optional branch), price, currency, observed_at, is_sale, is_member_price (default false), bonus_points (optional int), bulk_tiers (optional jsonb list of 1-3 {quantity 2-999, percent 1-99} tiers, applied on top of the entered regular price), tax_basis ('excl'|'incl'|'unknown', default 'unknown'), source, status, created_at)`; a composite foreign key `(shop_id, store_chain_id)` guarantees the branch belongs to the chain. Insert-only, never overwrite. Include `source` (='manual') and `status` (='active') now even with one value each — this is what lets AI/community sources slot in later without a migration touching every row.
- Current price = latest observation by date, always derived, never stored on the product. Lookups are chain-first: show the latest price per chain, most recent first; the branch is optional extra detail, not required anywhere.
- Times: `observed_at` stays `timestamptz`. The user enters Japan time with minutes (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`, saved as `...:00+09:00`). Always display with `timeZone: 'Asia/Tokyo'`. Order history by `observed_at` then `created_at`.

## Tax (the price is recorded as entered)
- A product may have a tax rate (`products.tax_rate`, 0 to 30 %, set with 税率 in the add forms and in 商品を編集; it applies to all its variations and 容量). For a price whose basis is 税抜 or 税不明, `components/PriceDisplay.tsx` shows a small calculator icon (`components/TaxHint.tsx`) next to the tax badge; tapping it shows a tooltip with the suggested tax-included price (`lib/prices/taxHint.ts`, rounded down, marked 未確認). Nothing is shown for 税込 prices, for a rate of 0, or when the product has no rate. The stored price is never changed.
- `tax_basis` is required on every entry: `excl` = 税抜き, `incl` = 税込み, `unknown` = 不明 (preselected). The price is stored exactly as entered. No tax rate is assumed or calculated anywhere (reduced rates and exceptions exist).
- Every price shows a small badge, via the `taxBasis` prop of `components/PriceDisplay.tsx`: 税抜 / 税込 / 税不明. Helpers live in `lib/prices/tax.ts` (`parseTaxBasis`, `canCompare`, `isCheapest`, `isWithinMonths`).
- Never declare a winner without full information: the 最安 badge appears on the price check page only when at least two chains have prices from the last 6 months and all of those share the same known basis (all 税抜 or all 税込). Otherwise there is no badge and no explanatory note. Older prices stay in the list but never get the badge.

## Prices, discounts and unit price
- Price block (Step 42; it replaces the older bracket and badge descriptions below where they differ): `components/PriceDisplay.tsx` draws ONE strip per price, with a small label above each price: 通常 (the entered price, shown once), then EVERY enabled coupon of the chain side by side (smallest percent first, `allDiscounts` in `lib/prices/coupon.ts`), and the membership percent (not when a coupon has the same percent). The best (largest) discount is the big coloured one: it is the price used for 最安, sorting and the price range filter (`bestDiscount` / `effectivePrice` are unchanged). The tax badge and the calculator icon (TaxHint) appear once, after the strip. The まとめ買い tiers and the ウェル活 points are small lines under the strip and do not repeat the regular price or the tax badge. A price that is already a member price shows one 会員価格 cell. The block leans right on price rows (`align="end"`, `className="ml-auto"` so it stays right when it wraps under the chain name) and left in the detail header (`align="start"`).
- Coupons: set per chain (`coupon_percents`), never stored on observations. Show the best enabled percent: coupon price = `floor(price × (100 − pct) / 100)`, large and rose-coloured with a `N%クーポン` badge, and the normal price in grey brackets `（通常価格 ¥…）`. It is an estimate (one item, tax not modelled). Always render prices with `components/PriceDisplay.tsx`.
- Membership (per chain, `member_discount_percent`): if the entered price is a normal price, show the membership price (same rounding) with the normal price in brackets; if `is_member_price` is true, the entered price is already the membership price, shown with a 会員価格 badge and no bracket. Discounts never stack: show the larger of the best coupon and the membership percent.
- Bonus points (per chain, `has_bonus_points`) never change the price: show a small `+Nポイント` text on the card and detail row only.
- まとめ買い (bulk discount, per chain `bulk_enabled`, set in the 店舗 chain options): for chains with it, price entry has a checkbox "this chain has bulk discount tiers (the entered price is the regular 1-item price)" and up to 3 rows of 個数 (2-999) and 割引率 (1-99); the first row is required. They are stored as `bulk_tiers` (jsonb list of 1-3 tiers sorted by quantity; each tier is {quantity, percent} (a percent off, 個以上) or {quantity, total} (a bundle price such as 5個で550円); every row of the form chooses 割引率 or 合計金額, and a bundle price must be below quantity x the regular price) on the observation. The rows are prefilled with the tiers used last at that chain (the same product first; `app/prices/bulkDefaults.ts`) until the user types. Helpers in `lib/prices/bulk.ts` (`parseBulkTiers`, `parseStoredTiers`, `bulkTiersToJson`, `bulkTierPrice` with the coupon rounding, down, `bulkItemPrice`, `bulkEquivalentPercent`, `bestBulkTier` = the tier with the lowest price per item). A bundle tier shows as `5個で ¥550（1個あたり ¥110・約8%引）` (`components/BulkTierLine.tsx`); its price per item is total / quantity with one decimal and the equivalent percent is rounded down. Display (`components/PriceDisplay.tsx`): the regular (or coupon) price as usual, plus a まとめ買い block listing each tier as `10個以上 20%引 ¥800（1個あたり）`. Bulk is display-only: it never changes 最安, sorting, the price range filter or the unit price, and it can be used together with a member price (nothing excludes it).
- ウェル活 (感謝デー, per chain: `point_use_enabled`, `point_use_multiplier`, set in the 店舗 chain options): on that day 1 point is worth 倍率 yen. A chain with it enabled shows an EXTRA small amber line under the price (Step 42: only the badge and the points, such as ウェル活 1,787ポイント; the regular price and the tax badge are not repeated; it used to read like this): `ウェル活 1,000ポイント（通常価格 ¥1,500）税込` (or 会員価格 when the entered price is a member price). Points = ceil(entered price ÷ 倍率) (`lib/prices/points.ts`). It never replaces the main price and never affects the 最安 badge, sorting, price range filter or unit price. The app does not check the date.
- Unit price (helpers in `lib/prices/unit.ts`, never stored): ml and L per 100 ml, g and kg per 100 g, 個 / 枚 / 袋 per piece. Computed from the discounted price (same estimate as the big price), shown with the tax label, on the product detail page (latest price card and each history row) and on every price row of the price check cards. Different kinds (ml vs g) are never compared.

## Demo mode and roles
- `profiles(id → auth.users, role text: admin or user, theme, display_name = optional nickname up to 30 characters, not unique, used for the greeting; users may update only theme and display_name)`: one row per account, created by a trigger. Users read only their own row; roles change only in the Supabase SQL Editor, never from the app. `is_admin()` is the SQL helper.
- `app_settings(id boolean, demo_mode boolean default false)`: one row. Anyone can read it; only admins can update it (RLS). `demo_mode_on()` is the SQL helper.
- While demo mode is on, visitors without an account can only READ (anon select policies on store_chains, shops, products, product_variations, product_packages, price_observations, store_chain_usage). Every write policy stays authenticated-only. New tables that visitors should see need their own "demo read" policy.
- `proxy.ts` lets visitors in only when the flag is on; if the flag cannot be read, demo mode counts as off.
- `lib/auth/viewer.ts` `getViewer()` gives one cached login check per request. Pages pass `readOnly = !loggedIn` down. Write controls stay visible but disabled: `components/ReadOnlyFieldset.tsx` (a disabled fieldset, renders children unchanged for logged-in users), the `disabled` prop of `DeleteObservationButton`, and the price entry Save buttons. Never hide pages from visitors.
- `components/ProfileMenu.tsx` (top right on every page except /login) opens a side menu: email, 店舗 (also for visitors), 設定 and ログアウト when logged in; a ログイン button for visitors. `/settings` shows the account and, for admins only, the デモモード toggle (`app/settings/actions.ts`).
- `getChainUsage(supabase, canRefresh)` skips the recount for visitors.

## Themes
- A theme is cosmetic only: colours, backgrounds, blur, borders. Functions and layout never change per theme.
- Themes are listed in `lib/themes/themes.ts` (id, label, preview colours). The user's choice is saved in `profiles.theme` (users can update only that column) and applied as `data-theme` on `<html>` in `app/layout.tsx`. Visitors and unknown values get the `default` theme.
- The default theme is the original design. Each other theme is its own plain CSS file `public/themes/<name>.css` (not processed by Tailwind: no @apply or @theme) with an `html[data-theme="<id>"]` block that redefines the panel tokens `--panel`, `--panel-soft`, `--chip`, `--line` (and may restyle the body background).
- UI rule: never hard-code `bg-white`, `border-stone-200`, `bg-stone-50` or `bg-stone-100` for panels, inputs, chips or borders. Use `bg-panel`, `border-line`, `bg-panel-soft`, `bg-chip`. Text greys and accent colours stay `stone-*` and `teal-*`.
- Themes so far: default, glass, sky (light blue/white), midnight (dark navy/lavender), calm (dark sea-green). Colour themes work by remapping Tailwind colour variables on html[data-theme]: the teal accent scale, the stone text greys, and for dark themes the amber/rose/red tints; panels use the --panel, --panel-soft, --chip, --line tokens. A NEW colour class used anywhere in the app (for example blue or green) must also be remapped in each theme file in public/themes/, or it will look wrong there. Contrast: after changing any colour or adding a theme, run `node scripts/contrast-audit.mjs` (it reads the real colours from the theme files and tests the price strip, badges, chips, bottom bar and buttons per theme). Every text/background pair must reach 4.5:1 (3:1 for large bold text) in every theme. Accepted exceptions: disabled controls (the fade is intended) and the default theme's teal-600 (white on the teal button and teal text on white, 3.66:1), kept on purpose. Add a new colour pair to the `checks` list in the script.
- Theme files are listed in the `css` field of `lib/themes/themes.ts` and `app/layout.tsx` links only those of the account's theme (React 19 `<link rel="stylesheet" precedence>`), so the default theme loads no extra file; the dark themes load `dark-base.css` (shared rules) plus their own file. Midnight also gives `rounded-2xl` panels a subtle glass look (highlight, top edge, light blur).
- Planned: animated backgrounds or moving characters per theme, as a fixed decoration layer behind the content that ignores taps (`pointer-events-none`), added in the layout and chosen by theme id.

## Deleting and category suggestions
- A 容量 (package) can be deleted only when it has no price entries (`deletePackage`, `app/products/deleteActions.ts`). A バリエーション can be deleted only when none of its 容量 has price entries; its 容量 are deleted with it (`delete_variation()` SQL function, one transaction). Any logged-in user may do this, with one confirm dialog. With price entries attached, only an admin can delete (same confirm dialog): `delete_package()` / `delete_variation()` then archive it as a tombstone row (kind package or variation) in `deleted_records` (rows stay, RLS hides them; revive by deleting the tombstone row; the identical 容量 or バリエーション cannot be re-added until the tombstone is removed). Regular users get 価格の記録があるため削除できません.
- Deleting a PRODUCT is admin only (`delete_product()` SQL function checks `is_admin()`), with one confirm dialog in the 商品を編集 area. It writes a tombstone row (kind product) to the single archive table `deleted_records` (record id, name fields, snapshot of variations, packages and price count). Packages and variations that an admin deletes while price entries are attached are archived in the same table (kind package or variation). While a tombstone exists, RLS hides the product, its variations, packages and price entries from every screen, including demo mode (`product_is_visible()`, `package_is_visible()`). The rows are NOT deleted. To revive: delete the tombstone row in the SQL Editor. For a permanent cleanup, delete the hidden price entries and rows by hand. Any new table that links to products or packages needs the same visibility rule.
- Category suggestions: `components/CategoryField.tsx` asks `suggestCategoryNames()` (`app/products/categoryActions.ts`); matching is in `lib/products/category.ts` (whole-term and single-kanji matches, up to 10, shown as name (number of products)).
- `components/ProductFields.tsx` holds the product fields (商品名, 英語名・別名, ブランド, カテゴリ, メモ) and is used by 商品を追加 and by 価格入力 > 新しい商品として登録 (prefix `np_`). Change fields there, never in one form only.

## Barcodes
- `product_barcodes(code PRIMARY KEY, product_id, package_id optional, created_at)`: a product can have up to 10 barcodes, a code belongs to one product (and optionally to one exact 容量 of it; the composite foreign key (package_id, product_id) keeps them consistent, and the barcode stays on the product if the 容量 is deleted). A barcode entered with a brand-new product on 価格入力 is linked to the 容量 created in the same form. Only the code text is stored, never an image. Codes are normalised by `lib/products/barcode.ts` (full-width to half-width, spaces and hyphens removed, upper case, 12-digit UPC stored as 13 digits). Barcodes of archived (deleted) products are hidden like the product, but the code stays reserved.
- The バーコード field (several rows, each with a camera button) is part of `components/ProductFields.tsx`, so 商品を追加 and 新しい商品として登録 both have it. Existing products: バーコード section in 商品を編集 (`app/products/ProductBarcodes.tsx`).
- `/scan` (home card バーコード, also a link on 価格入力): the camera reads the code on the device with the browser `BarcodeDetector` (Android Chrome; no library, nothing uploaded); where it is unsupported the page says so and a box takes the typed number (a USB scanner types into it). `lookupBarcode()` (`app/scan/actions.ts`): found → `/prices/new?product=<id>&package=<id if the barcode has a 容量>` (only that product 容量 listed, preselected when there is exactly one, the 新規 panel opens when it has none); not found → `/products?barcode=<code>` (商品を追加 opens with the code filled in; after saving, it continues to `/prices/new?product=<new id>`).

## Favorites
- `favorite_packages(user_id default auth.uid(), package_id, created_at, PRIMARY KEY (user_id, package_id))`: a favorite is one exact 容量 (packaging and variation), not a whole product; an account can favorite several sizes of the same product. RLS: an account reads, adds and removes only its own rows; there is NO anon policy, so demo mode visitors never see favorites; favorites of archived products, variations or packages are hidden (`package_is_visible`); a really deleted 容量 takes its favorites with it (on delete cascade).
- "Related" is never stored: a package that is not a favorite but shares the product AND the variation of a favorite (for a product without variations: the same product) is related (`lib/prices/favorites.ts`: `favoriteLevel`, `favoriteGroupKey`, with tests). `lib/prices/loadFavorites.ts` loads an account's favorites for the pages.
- `components/FavoriteButton.tsx`: a heart that toggles through `setFavorite()` (`app/check/favoriteActions.ts`, which revalidates `/check`), changing at once (`useOptimistic`) and going back if saving fails. Filled rose heart = favorite, faded outline = related, grey outline = other; tapping a related heart makes that size a favorite too. It sits to the left of the 容量 chip on every 容量 block of a 価格確認 card (a separate element on top of the card link, never inside the link) and in the header of the product detail page. Visitors get no heart and no styling.
- Card look on 価格確認 (`CARD_TONE` in `app/check/page.tsx`): favorite = soft rose border plus a faint rose tint fading from the left; related = half of the tint; other = unchanged. It uses only colours the themes remap (`rose-50`, `rose-200`; `rose-200` is remapped in `public/themes/dark-base.css`). Midnight's glass sheen replaces the tint (its background-image wins), so there the border and the heart carry the favorite look.
- 絞り込み has a お気に入りのみ checkbox (`fav=1`, only for logged-in accounts, counted in the filter number): it shows only the favorite packages, not the related ones, and a favorite with no price in the chosen period is still listed (with the usual no-price line).

## Admin tools
- Admin accounts (profiles.role = admin) see a 管理者ツール card at the bottom of the home page, leading to `/admin` (`app/admin/page.tsx`), a list of tools. Both `/admin` and its sub pages check `getViewer().isAdmin` on the server and redirect everyone else to the home page. Add a future tool by adding an entry to the `tools` list there and a page under `app/admin/`. Real admin actions must also be enforced in the database (admin check inside the SQL function or RLS policy), as `delete_product()` does.
- `/admin/units` (単位の言葉): manages the counter words of package units in the table `counter_units(unit PRIMARY KEY, label, enabled, sort_order)`. The measures ml, L, g, kg stay in code; every other unit is a counter word, stored as the word itself (the old keys count, sheet, bag keep their words 個, 枚, 袋) and shown per piece (`lib/prices/unitWords.ts`, used by packageLabel, unitPrice and the single-piece rule). The dropdowns on 価格入力 and the 容量 dialog list the measures plus the enabled words (`loadCounterUnits`, `lib/prices/counterUnits.ts`; fallback 個, 枚, 袋), and addPrice / addPackage refuse any other unit. Admins can hide or show a word, add a word (`lib/prices/counterWord.ts`: letters only, up to 8 characters, up to 30 words) and remove a word only when no 容量 uses it (`delete_counter_unit()`); words cannot be renamed; 個 can never be hidden or removed. Only admins can write (RLS, a column grant for enabled and sort_order, and the SQL functions).
- `/admin/suggestions` (検索のおすすめ): manages the quick-search chips on 価格確認. One-row table `search_suggestions(id boolean PRIMARY KEY, display_count 1-10 default 3, terms text[] up to 30, updated_at)`: readable by everyone logged in and by demo visitors, changeable only by admins (RLS plus a column grant for display_count, terms and updated_at; no insert or delete policy). The admin types the terms in one box separated by commas or line breaks (`lib/search/suggestions.ts`: tidied, repeats dropped, up to 30 terms of up to 30 characters, none of `( ) % _ * \`); the first `display_count` terms are shown (`loadVisibleSuggestions`, `lib/search/loadSuggestions.ts`) as chips under the search scope buttons (`app/check/SearchSuggestions.tsx`); tapping one opens `/check?q=<word>` (all fields) and the chip of the current search is highlighted. The admin page previews what is shown and what is held back by the count. Shown on 価格確認 and on the home page, not on the 商品 page.
- `/admin/brands` (ブランドの管理): brand consistency. Table `brand_aliases(id, brand, alias, created_at)` (brand = the spelling products should use, alias = another way to write it, unique on lower(alias); everyone reads, demo visitors read, only admins insert, update and delete). The brand box of 商品を追加, 新しい商品として登録 and 商品を編集 is `components/BrandField.tsx` (tap-to-fill suggestions like the category field, `suggestBrandNames` in `app/products/brandActions.ts`, `suggestBrands` in `lib/products/brand.ts`: brands found through an alias come first). On save, `resolveBrandText` (`lib/products/loadBrands.ts`) turns an alias into its brand and another spelling of a known brand into the known spelling, BEFORE the duplicate check (`createProduct`, `updateProduct` and the inline new product of 価格入力). The admin page lists every brand with its product count and aliases, the admin-only SQL function `merge_brand(p_from, p_to)` renames the brand of all matching products and keeps the old spelling as an alias (the まとめる form and the まとめる button of an alias that is still in use), and a warning lists products that are the same once aliases are applied (name and brand); merging those products is the planned `/admin/merge`. `search_products()` also searches `brand_aliases` in both directions (パスコ finds PASCO and PASCO finds パスコ).
- `/admin/merge` (商品の統合) is only a placeholder (準備中) so far.
- The home cards put the title and the description on separate lines (Android wrapped them badly).

## Search and the default 容量
- Product search must use `search_products()` (database, via `lib/search/products.ts`) on the server, and `matchesSearch()` (`lib/search/normalize.ts`) in the browser: both are width, case and hiragana/katakana insensitive, every word must match, and they cover name, 英語名・別名, brand, category, memo, variation values, barcodes and sizes. Never filter with plain `ilike` on a few columns again. On 価格確認 the scope buttons (すべて / 商品名 / カテゴリ, `lib/search/scope.ts`, `app/check/SearchScopeChips.tsx`) are a connected group inside the 絞り込み panel, on one line with お気に入りのみ, with a short hint line for the selected scope (the `hint` field of SEARCH_SCOPES); a scope other than すべて counts as an active filter (the number on the 絞り込み bar).
