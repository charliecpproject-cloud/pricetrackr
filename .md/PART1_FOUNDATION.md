# PART 1 — Foundation

## What this is
Private, single-user price tracker for Japan (JPY), bilingual JA/EN product data, installed as a web app on Android. Manual entry + price lookup with history. No AI, no other users yet. Phase 1 is complete and live on Vercel.

## Built (Phase 1 and early extras)
- Next.js on Vercel + Supabase (Auth/DB), owner-only account (sign-ups disabled), RLS on every table.
- Tables: products, product_variations, product_packages, store_chains, shops, price_observations, profiles, app_settings.
- Pages: home, price entry (with inline new product/package/branch), price check (latest price per chain), product detail (latest price, history, unit price), stores (chains, branches, coupons, membership and points settings), products (add/edit products, variations, packages, memo), delete own price entries.
- Also built: chain coupons, membership price and bonus points, product variations, product memo, tax basis, unit price on the detail page, app icon and web app manifest.

## Not built yet (OUT)
AI/photo extraction, freshness/trust scoring, translation-approval workflow, internet auto-research, multi-user, shopping list, favorites, alerts, gamification, community/regional features. See PART2/PART3 when relevant — don't build ahead of scope.

## Schema principles (don't flatten these)
- Product ≠ package: `products(id, name_ja, name_en, brand, category, memo (optional free text, max 1000 chars))` vs `product_packages(id, product_id, variation_id (optional), package_size, package_unit)`. A multi-pack is entered as one total size (6 × 350 ml = 2100 ml).
- Variations: `product_variations(id, product_id, attributes jsonb [{name, value}], attributes_key)`, user-defined and optional. `attributes_key` is canonical (NFKC, trimmed, lower-case, order-independent) and unique per product. A package may belong to a variation of its own product (composite FK `(variation_id, product_id)`). Prices always reference the exact package, so each package has its own history. To make different sizes share one history, use one package and put the detail in the price note. Helpers live in `lib/products/variation.ts`. Units: ml, L, g, kg, count (個), sheet (枚), bag (袋). Nested selects must follow real foreign keys: `price_observations` to `store_chains` (direct) and `shops`; `product_packages` to `products` and `product_variations`; `products` to `product_variations` and `product_packages`.
- Store chain ≠ shop: `store_chains(id, name, coupon_percents int[] default '{}', allowed values 5/10/15, member_discount_percent smallint null = no membership price, has_bonus_points bool default false, point_use_enabled bool default false, point_use_multiplier numeric(4,2) default 1.5)` vs `shops(id, store_chain_id, name, address, region)`. Chains also have `category` (a key from `lib/stores/category.ts`: supermarket, drugstore, convenience, discount, hundred_yen, apparel, other; null = 未分類). `store_chain_usage(store_chain_id, entries_last_month, updated_at)` is a lookup table of price entries per chain over the last 30 days (by observed_at), recounted by `refresh_store_chain_usage()` when the price entry page finds it empty or older than 7 days; it drives the price entry chain dropdown (grouped by category, most used first, top 10 unless すべて is ticked). Chains and branches with price entries cannot be deleted.
- Price = observation, not attribute: `price_observations(id, product_package_id, store_chain_id (required), shop_id (optional branch), price, currency, observed_at, is_sale, is_member_price (default false), bonus_points (optional int), tax_basis ('excl'|'incl'|'unknown', default 'unknown'), source, status, created_at)`; a composite foreign key `(shop_id, store_chain_id)` guarantees the branch belongs to the chain. Insert-only, never overwrite. Include `source` (='manual') and `status` (='active') now even with one value each — this is what lets AI/community sources slot in later without a migration touching every row.
- Current price = latest observation by date, always derived, never stored on the product. Lookups are chain-first: show the latest price per chain, most recent first; the branch is optional extra detail, not required anywhere.
- Times: `observed_at` stays `timestamptz`. The user enters Japan time with minutes (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`, saved as `...:00+09:00`). Always display with `timeZone: 'Asia/Tokyo'`. Order history by `observed_at` then `created_at`.

## Tax (the price is recorded as entered)
- `tax_basis` is required on every entry: `excl` = 税抜き, `incl` = 税込み, `unknown` = 不明 (preselected). The price is stored exactly as entered. No tax rate is assumed or calculated anywhere (reduced rates and exceptions exist).
- Every price shows a small badge, via the `taxBasis` prop of `components/PriceDisplay.tsx`: 税抜 / 税込 / 税不明. Helpers live in `lib/prices/tax.ts` (`parseTaxBasis`, `canCompare`, `isCheapest`, `isWithinMonths`).
- Never declare a winner without full information: the 最安 badge appears on the price check page only when at least two chains have prices from the last 6 months and all of those share the same known basis (all 税抜 or all 税込). Otherwise there is no badge and no explanatory note. Older prices stay in the list but never get the badge.

## Prices, discounts and unit price
- Coupons: set per chain (`coupon_percents`), never stored on observations. Show the best enabled percent: coupon price = `floor(price × (100 − pct) / 100)`, large and rose-coloured with a `N%クーポン` badge, and the normal price in grey brackets `（通常価格 ¥…）`. It is an estimate (one item, tax not modelled). Always render prices with `components/PriceDisplay.tsx`.
- Membership (per chain, `member_discount_percent`): if the entered price is a normal price, show the membership price (same rounding) with the normal price in brackets; if `is_member_price` is true, the entered price is already the membership price, shown with a 会員価格 badge and no bracket. Discounts never stack: show the larger of the best coupon and the membership percent.
- Bonus points (per chain, `has_bonus_points`) never change the price: show a small `+Nポイント` text on the card and detail row only.
- ウェル活 (感謝デー, per chain: `point_use_enabled`, `point_use_multiplier`, set in the 店舗 chain options): on that day 1 point is worth 倍率 yen. A chain with it enabled shows an EXTRA amber line under the price: `ウェル活 1,000ポイント（通常価格 ¥1,500）税込` (or 会員価格 when the entered price is a member price). Points = ceil(entered price ÷ 倍率) (`lib/prices/points.ts`). It never replaces the main price and never affects the 最安 badge, sorting, price range filter or unit price. The app does not check the date.
- Unit price (helpers in `lib/prices/unit.ts`, never stored): ml and L per 100 ml, g and kg per 100 g, 個 / 枚 / 袋 per piece. Computed from the discounted price (same estimate as the big price), shown with the tax label, and only on the product detail page (latest price card and each history row). Different kinds (ml vs g) are never compared.

## Demo mode and roles
- `profiles(id → auth.users, role text: admin or user)`: one row per account, created by a trigger. Users read only their own row; roles change only in the Supabase SQL Editor, never from the app. `is_admin()` is the SQL helper.
- `app_settings(id boolean, demo_mode boolean default false)`: one row. Anyone can read it; only admins can update it (RLS). `demo_mode_on()` is the SQL helper.
- While demo mode is on, visitors without an account can only READ (anon select policies on store_chains, shops, products, product_variations, product_packages, price_observations, store_chain_usage). Every write policy stays authenticated-only. New tables that visitors should see need their own "demo read" policy.
- `proxy.ts` lets visitors in only when the flag is on; if the flag cannot be read, demo mode counts as off.
- `lib/auth/viewer.ts` `getViewer()` gives one cached login check per request. Pages pass `readOnly = !loggedIn` down. Write controls stay visible but disabled: `components/ReadOnlyFieldset.tsx` (a disabled fieldset, renders children unchanged for logged-in users), the `disabled` prop of `DeleteObservationButton`, and the price entry Save buttons. Never hide pages from visitors.
- `components/ProfileMenu.tsx` (top right on every page except /login) opens a side menu: email, 設定 and ログアウト when logged in; a ログイン button for visitors. `/settings` shows the account and, for admins only, the デモモード toggle (`app/settings/actions.ts`).
- `getChainUsage(supabase, canRefresh)` skips the recount for visitors.

## Themes
- A theme is cosmetic only: colours, backgrounds, blur, borders. Functions and layout never change per theme.
- Themes are listed in `lib/themes/themes.ts` (id, label, preview colours). The user's choice is saved in `profiles.theme` (users can update only that column) and applied as `data-theme` on `<html>` in `app/layout.tsx`. Visitors and unknown values get the `default` theme.
- The default theme is the original design. Each other theme is an `html[data-theme="<id>"]` block at the end of `app/globals.css` that redefines the panel tokens `--panel`, `--panel-soft`, `--chip`, `--line` (and may restyle the body background).
- UI rule: never hard-code `bg-white`, `border-stone-200`, `bg-stone-50` or `bg-stone-100` for panels, inputs, chips or borders. Use `bg-panel`, `border-line`, `bg-panel-soft`, `bg-chip`. Text greys and accent colours stay `stone-*` and `teal-*`.
- Themes so far: default, glass, sky (light blue/white), midnight (dark navy/lavender), calm (dark sea-green). Colour themes work by remapping Tailwind colour variables on html[data-theme]: the teal accent scale, the stone text greys, and for dark themes the amber/rose/red tints; panels use the --panel, --panel-soft, --chip, --line tokens. A NEW colour class used anywhere in the app (for example blue or green) must also be remapped in each theme block in globals.css, or it will look wrong there.
- Planned: animated backgrounds or moving characters per theme, as a fixed decoration layer behind the content that ignores taps (`pointer-events-none`), added in the layout and chosen by theme id.

## Deleting and category suggestions
- A 容量 (package) can be deleted only when it has no price entries (`deletePackage`, `app/products/deleteActions.ts`). A バリエーション can be deleted only when none of its 容量 has price entries; its 容量 are deleted with it (`delete_variation()` SQL function, one transaction). Any logged-in user may do this, with one confirm dialog. With price entries attached, only an admin can delete (same confirm dialog): `delete_package()` / `delete_variation()` then archive it as a tombstone row (kind package or variation) in `deleted_records` (rows stay, RLS hides them; revive by deleting the tombstone row; the identical 容量 or バリエーション cannot be re-added until the tombstone is removed). Regular users get 価格の記録があるため削除できません.
- Deleting a PRODUCT is admin only (`delete_product()` SQL function checks `is_admin()`), with one confirm dialog in the 商品を編集 area. It writes a tombstone row (kind product) to the single archive table `deleted_records` (record id, name fields, snapshot of variations, packages and price count). Packages and variations that an admin deletes while price entries are attached are archived in the same table (kind package or variation). While a tombstone exists, RLS hides the product, its variations, packages and price entries from every screen, including demo mode (`product_is_visible()`, `package_is_visible()`). The rows are NOT deleted. To revive: delete the tombstone row in the SQL Editor. For a permanent cleanup, delete the hidden price entries and rows by hand. Any new table that links to products or packages needs the same visibility rule.
- Category suggestions: `components/CategoryField.tsx` asks `suggestCategoryNames()` (`app/products/categoryActions.ts`); matching is in `lib/products/category.ts` (whole-term and single-kanji matches, up to 10, shown as name (number of products)).
- `components/ProductFields.tsx` holds the product fields (商品名, 英語名・別名, ブランド, カテゴリ, メモ) and is used by 商品を追加 and by 価格入力 > 新しい商品として登録 (prefix `np_`). Change fields there, never in one form only.

## Products page
- Default view: the 3 most recently added products under the heading 最近追加した商品; the button すべての商品を表示 (`?all=1`) shows all (max 300); a search (`?q=`) shows up to 20 matches.
- On each card, the buttons バリエーションを追加 and 容量を追加 open ONE shared popup (`app/products/ProductDialogs.tsx`, provider around the list, `useProductDialogs()`), with the product preselected; the popup closes after a successful add. There are no product dropdown forms at the bottom of the page any more (only 商品を追加).

## Delete buttons
- Every delete button on 商品 shows how many price records are attached (価格の記録 N件, or 件数不明 if the count could not be loaded). For an admin with records attached the button reads 上書き削除 and the confirm dialog says it is an admin override (records stay but are hidden). A regular user with records attached gets no dialog, only the error message. Counts come from `package_price_counts()` (loaded once per page in `app/products/page.tsx`).
- On 商品, the すべての商品を表示 / 最近の商品だけ表示 button and 商品を追加 sit at the top, above the cards.

## Barcodes
- `product_barcodes(code PRIMARY KEY, product_id, package_id optional, created_at)`: a product can have up to 10 barcodes, a code belongs to one product (and optionally to one exact 容量 of it; the composite foreign key (package_id, product_id) keeps them consistent, and the barcode stays on the product if the 容量 is deleted). A barcode entered with a brand-new product on 価格入力 is linked to the 容量 created in the same form. Only the code text is stored, never an image. Codes are normalised by `lib/products/barcode.ts` (full-width to half-width, spaces and hyphens removed, upper case, 12-digit UPC stored as 13 digits). Barcodes of archived (deleted) products are hidden like the product, but the code stays reserved.
- The バーコード field (several rows, each with a camera button) is part of `components/ProductFields.tsx`, so 商品を追加 and 新しい商品として登録 both have it. Existing products: バーコード section in 商品を編集 (`app/products/ProductBarcodes.tsx`).
- `/scan` (home card バーコード, also a link on 価格入力): the camera reads the code on the device with the browser `BarcodeDetector` (Android Chrome; no library, nothing uploaded); where it is unsupported the page says so and a box takes the typed number (a USB scanner types into it). `lookupBarcode()` (`app/scan/actions.ts`): found → `/prices/new?product=<id>&package=<id if the barcode has a 容量>` (only that product 容量 listed, preselected when there is exactly one, the 新規 panel opens when it has none); not found → `/products?barcode=<code>` (商品を追加 opens with the code filled in; after saving, it continues to `/prices/new?product=<new id>`).

## Admin tools
- Admin accounts (profiles.role = admin) see a 管理者ツール card at the bottom of the home page, leading to `/admin` (`app/admin/page.tsx`), a list of tools. Both `/admin` and its sub pages check `getViewer().isAdmin` on the server and redirect everyone else to the home page. Add a future tool by adding an entry to the `tools` list there and a page under `app/admin/`. Real admin actions must also be enforced in the database (admin check inside the SQL function or RLS policy), as `delete_product()` does.
- `/admin/merge` (商品の統合) is only a placeholder (準備中) so far.
- The home cards put the title and the description on separate lines (Android wrapped them badly).

Per CONVENTIONS.md: RLS everywhere, secrets in env vars only, parameterized queries only, validate the entry form's input. No exceptions for single-user.

## Tech stack (locked)

Supabase via `@supabase/ssr` only: `createBrowserClient` in the browser (stores session in cookies), `createServerClient` on the server/middleware. Never `createClient` from `@supabase/supabase-js` for app code. Server auth checks use `getUser()`, not `getSession()`. Before importing from a helper file, read its actual exports; don't guess names. CLI migrations committed to Git, no dashboard schema edits. Tailwind + shadcn/ui, lucide-react. react-hook-form + Zod v4 (use `error.issues[0].message`; `error.errors` no longer exists). No `next/font`: the Japanese system font stack is set in `app/globals.css`.

## UI style
- Layout: mobile-first, one centered column. The column width and side padding come ONLY from `<main>` in `app/layout.tsx` (`mx-auto w-full max-w-xl px-4`). Pages must not add their own `max-w-*`, `mx-auto`, `px-*`, `min-h-screen` or background wrapper.
- Look: warm off-white background, one teal accent (`teal-600`, set as shadcn `--primary`), white cards (`rounded-2xl border border-stone-200 shadow-sm`), generous spacing, bold large prices with `tabular-nums`, lucide-react icons. One grey palette: `stone-*` only (never `gray` or `slate`); errors `text-red-600`, success `text-teal-700`.
- Navigation: bottom tab bar (ホーム, 価格入力, 価格確認, 店舗, 商品); the active tab has a teal pill behind its icon and a bold label.
- Controls: inputs and buttons at least 48px tall (`h-12`), input text 16px or larger. Exceptions on purpose: small label-style rows (collapsible 支店) and the tax radio tiles (`h-10`). Use shadcn `Button`, `Card`, `Input`, `Label`, `Badge` from `@/components/ui/*`; keep native `<select>` for dropdowns. Installed shadcn components are only: button, card, input, label, badge, textarea. Never import any other `@/components/ui/*` file; use a native element instead.
- Forms: every field is marked 必須 or 任意 with a badge. `Badge`, `Field`, `NewToggle`, `controlCls` and `labelCls` live in `components/FormParts.tsx`. No "必須 は必ず入力してください" explanation line. Rarely used fields are collapsed by default with `components/Collapsible.tsx` (the content stays mounted, so its inputs are still submitted).
- Every page has a heading, an empty state, and a visible error message. Import project files with the `@/` alias, never long `../` paths. No new dependencies.

### Page specifics
- Home: compact teal gradient bar with the app icon and "PriceTrackr" (no headline text), two large action cards (価格入力, 価格確認); no logout link (it is in the profile menu).
- Price entry (order): product search, product and package (the 新規 toggle creates a product and package inline, fields `np_*`; an exact duplicate product is refused, never merged), chain, 支店 (collapsed, optional, with its own 新規 toggle), 価格 in a short box with a 特売 chip beside it, the member-price checkbox directly under the price (only for chains with a membership price, checked by default; it means the entered price is already the membership price), tax radio (Tag icon 税抜き, Receipt icon 税込み, HelpCircle icon 不明; 不明 preselected), bonus points (only for chains with points), 日時, メモ, 保存. A round floating Save button (floppy-disk icon) appears at the bottom right while the main Save button is off screen, with a short message at the top after saving. A round 連続入力 (continuous entry) toggle sits at the bottom left; when on, a save keeps chain, branch, tax, sale flag and member-price checkbox, clears price, memo, bonus points, the product and its search (cursor goes back to the search box) and sets the time to now; when off, a save resets tax, member price and bonus points as before. The mode resets when leaving the page. The form is submitted by hand (onSubmit with startTransition), so the browser never clears fields by itself.
- Price check: search, then one card per package with the latest price per chain (most recent first, tax badge on each price, 最安 only under the tax rules above). No unit price on these cards.
- Product detail: 最新価格 card and the price history, each with tax badge and unit price line, and delete for own entries.
- Stores: a dropdown chooses one chain (`?chain=<id>`); only that chain's branches are loaded; the add-chain and add-branch forms are collapsed `<details>`.
- Products: search first (`?q=`), 20 products at a time; the add forms (商品, バリエーション, 容量) are collapsed `<details>` below the list.

## UI language
Japanese only, hardcoded strings, no i18n library, polite です/ます tone. Show `name_ja` first, falling back to `name_en`. A product has ONE main name field (商品名, any language, no language detection), stored in the `name_ja` column, plus an optional 英語名・別名 alias stored in `name_en` and used in searches. The column names are historical. Products are always referenced by id, so renaming never breaks prices. Never translate code identifiers, form field names, database columns, or stored values (source, status, currency, tax_basis). Terms: ホーム, 価格入力, 価格確認, 店舗, チェーン, 支店, 商品, 容量, 価格, 特売, 日時, メモ, 保存, 削除, 検索, ログイン, ログアウト, メールアドレス, パスワード, クーポン, 通常価格, 会員価格, 最安, 税抜き, 税込み, 不明 (radio), 税抜 / 税込 / 税不明 (badges), 商品バリエーション, パッケージ. Unit `count` displays as 個.
